Strategic implant placement in the pterygoid region of the maxilla for patients with bone loss, avoiding grafts and reducing treatment time.

  • Faster recovery
  • High success rate

Smile Corrections

See how teeth are corrected

Animated previews of common orthodontic problems and how treatment fixes them.

Crossbite

Upper teeth sit inside lower teeth. Treatment widens the arch and aligns the bite.

Overbite

Upper front teeth overlap lower teeth too much. Braces bring them into balance.

Crowding

Teeth overlap due to lack of space. Braces/aligners create room and straighten.

Spacing / Gaps

Unwanted gaps between teeth. Aligners close gaps for a uniform smile.
